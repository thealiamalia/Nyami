Terms of Service

How Nyami may be used, what it provides, and how to get support.

Effective October 18, 2026

Who operates Nyami

Nyami is a Discord bot operated by thealiamalia, the Nyami operator. Contact the operator at Nyami’s Discord support server for support, abuse reports, or questions about these terms. These terms take effect on October 18, 2026.

What the service does

Nyami provides slash commands for image searches, linked video search results, Twitch stream tracking, and configured OBS controls. Available commands depend on the bot's configuration and permissions in your server. Twitch stream cards use periodically updated previews and links to Twitch; they are not a custom live-video player. Video search results link to their source rather than guaranteeing playback inside Discord.

Search results come from third parties. Relevance, availability, image quality, stream-status timing, and continued access to external content can change. Search filtering and moderation options do not guarantee that every result will suit every audience.

Who may use Nyami

You must meet Discord's applicable age and account requirements, comply with the rules of the server where you use Nyami, and be permitted to access any third-party content you request. Adding Nyami to a server requires the appropriate Discord server permissions.

Acceptable use

Use Nyami in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, these terms, applicable law, and the rules of the services whose content you access. Do not use Nyami to obtain or distribute unlawful or exploitative content, harass people, infringe others' rights, expose private information, spam, evade access controls, or interfere with the bot or other services.

Server operators are responsible for choosing appropriate channels and permissions and for moderating content posted in their servers. Permission-restricted commands may only be used by authorized people. Current Twitch management commands require a server moderator or administrator. Ordinary channels and direct messages allow Strict searches only. Moderate requires an age-restricted server channel; Off also requires moderator or administrator permissions. Private replies use the same channel gate. OBS controls require the single owner bound to that configured profile as well as server management permissions; an optional trusted-server restriction can narrow access further. Provider filters do not guarantee lawful or age-appropriate results, and other provider or platform restrictions can still apply.

Third-party content and rights

Nyami is an independent bot and is not endorsed by Discord, Twitch, Microsoft, Google, or the operators of search-result websites. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. Images, videos, stream previews, titles, and other third-party material belong to their respective rights holders. A search result or bot download does not grant you a license to reuse or redistribute it. Nyami uploads individual images only when the operator has recorded appropriate redistribution permission and attribution information; otherwise it returns source links. Use the support contact to report infringement or request a takedown.

Privacy and stored information

The Nyami’s Privacy Policy describes processing of command information, Discord identifiers, search requests, local image archives, delivery history, logs, browser search data, and Twitch tracking records. Private Discord replies can still produce records and files on the bot operator's computer.

Availability and changes

Nyami runs when the operator's configured systems and required third-party services are available. Commands and integrations may be modified, restricted, suspended, or discontinued. Search and Twitch statistics reflect the information available to the bot at the time; Twitch viewer summaries are sampled observations rather than complete historical analytics.

The operator may restrict access to address misuse or operational problems. A server administrator may remove the bot from their server. Removing the bot does not automatically erase its existing local archives or logs; use the Privacy Policy's contact process to request deletion.

Updated terms and contact

The operator will publish material changes to these terms on this page with an updated effective date. Contact Nyami’s Discord support server with questions, reports, or requests relating to Nyami.

Talk to Nyami’s operator

For support, privacy questions, or a data request, join the support server and ask to contact the Nyami operator. Do not post passwords, tokens, or private request details publicly.
