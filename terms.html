Terms of Service

How Nyami may be used, what it provides, and how to get support.

Operator and agreement

Nyami is a Discord bot operated by thealiamalia. Contact Nyami's Discord support server for support, abuse reports, copyright concerns, or questions about these terms. Ask to contact the operator privately; do not post sensitive request details in public channels.

By using Nyami, you agree to these terms and to follow the rules that apply to your use of Discord and connected services. If you do not agree, stop using the bot. These terms do not replace Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, or applicable law.

Features and configuration

Nyami provides image, video, meme and web searches; Twitch cards and authorized broadcaster tools; owner-bound OBS controls; music playback; games; profiles; saved messages and reminders; community tools; moderation; administration; and privacy controls. Available features depend on the instance's configuration, dependencies and current permissions.

Configured AI features include conversation, translation, summarization, uploaded-image analysis, and original image generation. In staff-enabled channels, Nyami may reply without a mention. Separately enabled proactive conversation can start or continue a topical exchange, including more than one message without a new human reply. Mention replies and private commands have their own entry points. Staff can disable automatic channel chat; individuals can opt out of AI participation.

Natural-language requests may invoke supported command functions, such as finding a picture, video, meme or web link, or explicitly drawing an image. Optional conversation discoveries can add a relevant search result without a slash command. They retain the applicable permission and participation checks. Model output does not grant permission to perform privileged Discord, Twitch or OBS actions. Some operations require a separate command or confirmation.

Eligibility and channel rules

You must meet Discord's minimum age and the minimum age required in your country, comply with your server's rules, and be entitled to access content you request. Adding or configuring the bot requires the relevant server permissions.

Adult features require age 18 or older and any applicable Discord age restrictions. Fictional-romance preferences require explicit user opt-in and adult self-confirmation; this is not identity verification. An opt-in does not override channel rules, permissions, platform restrictions or the law.

Ordinary channels and direct messages allow Strict image/video searches only. Moderate requires an age-restricted server channel; Off additionally requires current moderator or administrator permission. Private replies remain subject to the originating channel's checks. Chat romance preferences may request Off for image/video only when those same conditions are met. Meme and web searches stay Strict. Original image generation uses its own prompt/output checks and does not offer SafeSearch Off. Filtering reduces risk but cannot guarantee that every result is suitable.

Acceptable use and authorized actions

Do not use Nyami for unlawful or exploitative content, child sexual abuse material, non-consensual intimate material, harassment, hate or threats, fraud, privacy violations, infringement, spam, or evasion of access controls. Do not use automated conversation to fabricate engagement or flood channels. Do not bypass provider verification, rate limits, permissions or other security controls.

You must have permission to submit, analyze, quote, transform or redistribute other people's text, images and media. Do not submit passwords, tokens, confidential material or sensitive personal information you are not authorized to share. Server staff are responsible for appropriate channel placement, feature configuration and moderation.

Twitch command management is restricted to moderators/administrators; broadcaster changes additionally need the configured broadcaster authorization and owner checks. OBS commands require the single owner bound to that profile, server-management permission and any configured trusted-server restriction. Other users need their own separately configured profile or instance. Music and other actions remain subject to their command permissions and provider availability. Giveaway hosts supply their own prizes; Nyami does not collect entry payments or guarantee fulfillment.

AI character and generated content

Nyami's cat-girl persona, emotions and relationships are fictional AI character behavior, not a human identity or a real-world relationship. Participation and optional romance can be turned off. Stored conversation recall is not perfect memory, learning new model weights, or a promise to accept every request.

AI responses, descriptions, transcriptions, summaries, translations and generated images can be incorrect, incomplete or misleading. Search excerpts are not independently verified facts. Verify important information and do not treat Nyami as professional advice or a reliable way to identify people, establish consent, or infer sensitive personal attributes from appearance. Image analysis supports uploaded still images and the first frame of supported animations; it does not analyze an entire video.

Generated images are marked as AI-generated. You remain responsible for how you use or share outputs. Nyami does not promise originality, exclusivity, copyright ownership or freedom from third-party rights in generated material. Prompt and output checks can reject a generation and do not guarantee that every output is accurate or appropriate.

Third-party content and availability

Nyami is independent and is not endorsed by Discord, Twitch, search providers, model publishers or source websites. Those services have separate terms and privacy practices. Third-party images, videos, music, stream previews and other material remain subject to their respective rights. A result, preview, download or upload does not itself grant a license to reuse it.

Image searches generally return remote previews and source links. Selected videos may be temporarily downloaded and converted for a complete MP4 attachment when permitted and technically possible; other results remain previews or links. Twitch cards contain periodically updated information and links, not a custom embedded live-stream player. Availability, relevance, freshness and playback inside Discord are not guaranteed. Local AI processing may take minutes, and services can require verification or refuse access.

Privacy and controls

Read the Privacy Policy for processing, recipients, retention and deletion details. AI conversation memory is enabled by default for participating users and stored locally in encrypted runtime storage until erased. Private replies are not a promise of zero retention by Nyami or Discord.

Use /chat memory enabled:False to erase saved chat and opt out of lasting memory, /chat forget to erase it once, and /nyami preferences to control AI participation and conversation discoveries. /privacy preview, /privacy export and /privacy delete provide controls over your own local records. Already-posted Discord messages, browser caches, legacy files and independent backups need separate handling where applicable.

Service changes, suspension and contact

Nyami is provided as available. Features, limits and integrations may change, fail, be suspended or be discontinued. The operator may restrict access to address misuse or operational problems. These terms do not exclude rights or responsibilities that applicable law does not permit to be excluded.

Server administrators can remove the bot. Removal clears eligible server-scoped runtime records through the implemented cleanup, but does not erase messages already posted to Discord or every independent copy. Contact support about records needing manual review.

Material changes will be published with an updated effective date. The policy update notes summarize this revision. Contact Nyami's support server with questions or requests.

Talk to Nyami’s operator

For support, privacy questions, or a data request, join the support server and ask to contact the Nyami operator. Do not post passwords, tokens, or private request details publicly.
