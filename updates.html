Policy Updates

What changed in Nyami's policies and where to find your controls.

Effective and last updated October 21, 2026

Published revision: October 21, 2026. The Terms and Privacy Policy were originally effective October 18, 2026.

October 21, 2026

  • Added local AI conversation, natural-language feature requests, automatic channel replies and separately enabled proactive conversation.
  • Made default-on lasting conversation memory, scoped recall, retention and opt-out/erase/export controls explicit.
  • Added uploaded-image analysis, local image generation, temporary encrypted processing and the difference between local cleanup and Discord attachment retention.
  • Updated image/video, meme and web-search disclosures, including external queries from optional conversation discoveries and browser caches.
  • Expanded coverage of music, games, profiles, community/staff tools, Twitch and owner-bound OBS integrations.
  • Clarified fictional AI character/adult preferences, permissions, source rights, content checks and limitations. Adult self-confirmation is not identity verification.
  • Removed outdated descriptions of a new permanent image archive, video-link-only delivery, disabled-only AI and manual-only privacy controls.

Your controls

Use /chat memory enabled:False to erase saved chat and opt out of lasting memory, /chat forget to erase it once, and /nyami preferences for AI participation and optional discoveries. Use /privacy preview, /privacy export and /privacy delete for supported personal records. Staff can disable automatic/proactive channel chat. Public Discord messages and independent copies require separate handling.

Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the complete policies. For questions or a private data request, contact Nyami support.

Talk to Nyami’s operator

For support, privacy questions, or a data request, join the support server and ask to contact the Nyami operator. Do not post passwords, tokens, or private request details publicly.
