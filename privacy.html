Privacy Policy

What Nyami processes, where it is stored, and how to make a data request.

Effective and last updated October 22, 2026 · R2

Effective and last updated: October 22, 2026. This revision replaces the policy originally effective October 18, 2026.

Operator and contact

Nyami is operated by thealiamalia. For support, access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, join Nyami's Discord support server and ask to contact the operator privately. Do not post passwords, tokens, ID documents or sensitive request details publicly.

This policy covers Nyami's bot processing and policy website. Discord and connected services handle information under their own policies. A private or ephemeral bot reply limits its audience in Discord; it does not mean that no processing or retention occurs.

October 22 R2 safeguards and operational follow-up

Generated text, including translations, summaries and image descriptions, passes an additional local safety model and supplementary threat check. Prompts and drafts stay on the operator's computer for this check and are not sent to its publisher. Checks can miss or misclassify content; unavailable or invalid checks stop generated delivery. Recognized private medical/financial disclosures, identifiers and credentials are rejected before AI processing or supported free-form storage. Do not submit sensitive documents in uploaded images. General educational and identity discussion remains supported.

Automated browser/HTML/SearXNG result collection and media extraction are paused unless the operator has recorded current permission for the exact service host and operation, including upstream review where relevant. No such provider permission is assumed from owning a video or opening a page. Manual source/search links can remain available. Video sources with unknown/invalid ratings or known adult ratings cannot produce downloaded attachments; unchecked source links suppress automatic previews.

Reminder cancellation and deletion invalidate unsent work and prevent erased records being recreated. Privacy choices of original message authors are rechecked after queuing and before processing/delivery. Already transmitted requests cannot necessarily be recalled.

Confirmed /privacy delete also attempts to scrub the user's records from recognized Nyami-created local database backups. Unreadable snapshots remain pending. It retains a minimal user-ID/date/status receipt for the operator to review browser/cache, legacy logs, posted Discord messages and external copies. Pending reviews remain until handled; completed receipts expire within 30 days. Owner-only /privacy requests and /privacy resolve manage this follow-up; resolving a receipt does not itself erase outside data. Independent copies and other services' retention still require individual handling. Contact support privately for completion details.

Commands, messages and identifiers

Nyami processes Discord user, server, channel, interaction and message identifiers; names and supplied command options; permission information; timestamps; and delivery/error information needed to operate configured features. Discord's connection may deliver events from channels the bot can access. AI replies use eligible messages in staff-enabled chat channels, direct mentions, private chat commands and supported image/reply requests; the bot does not build an indiscriminate transcript of every accessible channel.

Message actions can process the message you select, its author/link and your requested operation, such as bookmarking, quoting, reminding, translating, summarizing or reporting. Reports, feedback, appeals and support threads may disclose the submitted information to the server staff responsible for handling them. Public bot replies, profiles, game moves, votes and other shared contributions are visible according to their Discord destination.

Routine command logs record command names, timing, limited request diagnostics and errors rather than raw search phrases or routine requester/server IDs. Legacy logs or exceptional dependency errors may contain identifiers, URLs, filenames or other diagnostic details. They need operator review when handling a privacy request.

AI chat and conversation memory

The current AI setup uses local Ollama for text conversation and a separate local vision model for image analysis. Text prompts and selected conversation context are processed on the operator's computer. Nyami does not use these conversations to train or fine-tune model weights. Remembering an exchange means retrieving stored text for a later response.

Lasting memory is off until you explicitly enable it with /chat memory enabled:True. The current memory feature stores only approved, simple animal, hobby, music, flower or color preferences, normalized into a short preference and neutral acknowledgement. It does not archive free-form conversations, model replies, private or sensitive disclosures, or inferred identity/relationship profiles. Stored preferences have user/server/channel scope and timestamps; private command chat has its own scope. Recall is bounded and does not expose another user's records.

Preferences expire within 30 days, or sooner when erased or eligible server cleanup applies. Earlier memory settings do not count as a new opt-in. On startup under the normal Windows account, the updated code removes old raw chat archives except approved preferences, which are normalized and given the same maximum age. This sanitation covers the active runtime database, not independent backups or historical browser files. Encryption does not conceal records from the authorized operator.

  • /chat memory enabled:False erases saved chat and turns lasting memory off. Turning it on again permits future storage.
  • /chat forget or /chat reset clears your saved/recent chat without changing your lasting-memory preference.
  • /nyami preferences can turn AI participation off; AI opt-out clears the associated chat memory and pending responses.
  • /privacy preview, /privacy export memory_page:1 and /privacy delete provide the additional controls described below.

Disabling lasting memory does not disable the processing necessary to answer a new request, command diagnostics, or records separately needed for another feature.

Automatic replies, proactive conversation and searches

Staff can enable or disable ordinary automatic replies in specified channels; mentions and private commands are separate entry points. There are no idle-channel conversation starters or activity-maintenance messages. A separately staff-enabled feature may deliver one topical follow-up after a person explicitly requests it. It stops after that contribution, an intervening message, cancellation or a failed attempt; it does not repeatedly retry or keep speaking without fresh engagement. Old proactive settings need to be re-enabled for this behavior.

Automatic conversation discoveries are off until both staff and the individual enable them. /nyami preferences chat_discovery:True expressly permits future short topic queries derived from that person's eligible messages to be shared with Bing, Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave, or the configured SearXNG service and its upstream engines. The choice, policy version, recipients and time are recorded locally. Existing older preferences require a fresh choice. The general channel transcript is not sent. Staff use /chat discoveries; individuals can revoke with /nyami preferences chat_discovery:False. Revocation cancels pending discoveries and clears their short-lived context.

An explicit search or media request separately directs the requested query to the configured search providers. Automatic discovery context expires after 30 idle minutes; successful-query/requester timestamps expire after six hours. These process-memory caches clear on restart or relevant reset/opt-out. Current requested-follow-up context is temporary and is not a durable raw-conversation archive. Search excerpts may be processed by the selected text backend only when its sharing requirements are satisfied.

Uploaded images and local image generation

Image analysis: uploaded Discord attachments, or eligible same-channel reply attachments, are retrieved from Discord's CDN, decoded and resized in memory, then supplied to the local vision model with the question and selected scoped text context. Up to three images can be processed; supported animations provide their first frame only. Raw pixels and attachment URLs are not added to Nyami's chat-memory archive. Questions, image counts, pixels and textual descriptions are not stored as raw conversation memories; only separately supported approved preferences can become lasting memory. The original upload and any delivered answer remain subject to Discord's storage. /chat analyze answers privately; automatic channel analysis follows the channel's visibility. Current access and participation checks also apply to the original sender of a referenced image.

Original image generation: /imagine and explicit supported drawing requests process a description and optional/random seed with a local image generator and local output checker. Prompts and generated images are temporarily written under encrypted local runtime storage. Normal completion, failure and cancellation remove the temporary job. A sudden process or computer crash can leave encrypted job files requiring operator cleanup. The generator does not create a permanent Image/Video archive or send generation prompts to a hosted inference provider. Delivered images are marked AI-generated, include their seed and have rendering metadata removed. Generation requests and delivery notes are not saved as raw chat memories; the slash generation command does not add one either. Discord retains delivered attachments independently.

Local model and software installation downloads public files from their publishers. These downloads are separate from sending a user's prompt or image to hosted inference. Local processing and content checks do not guarantee accurate or appropriate results.

Search media, browser state and external recipients

Image, video, meme and web searches process phrases, filters, engine selections, source URLs, titles, preview/thumbnail URLs and selected excerpts. Repeat prevention stores scoped URL identities/hashes and delivery timestamps, without downloading image bytes to fingerprint them. Browser Auto/fallback and broad discovery may send related queries or source/date variants to several engines, including Bing, Google, DuckDuckGo and Brave, or a configured SearXNG service and its upstream engines. Meme discovery uses selected finished-meme sources such as Imgflip; captioning supplied images processes those images in memory.

Image-search results use remote previews and source links rather than a new permanent image archive. Discord and the browser may fetch/cache images independently. For supported selected videos, Nyami may temporarily download streams and upload a compatible MP4 only when the application owner has recorded permission for that exact source, video purpose, evidence, required attribution and an expiry date. No current permission means source-link/preview delivery without a video download. Approval is an operator rights determination, not an automatic license verification. Temporary inputs, processing logs and outputs are encrypted locally and removed after sending, fallback, failure or cancellation. Recognized incomplete video jobs are cleaned on the next video-preparation attempt. Unsupported results remain links/previews. Neither local cleanup nor an ephemeral reply erases Discord's copies.

The dedicated Vivaldi profile can retain search history, cookies, cache, preferences and site sessions until the operator clears them. Search providers and source websites receive the queries and network requests needed to resolve, preview, stream or process results, and may retain them under their own policies. Clicking a source link also connects you directly to that service.

Other configured features

  • Twitch: public broadcaster IDs/logins, profiles, stream/category/title/tag metadata, artwork/preview URLs, observed changes, sampled viewers and timestamps; Discord destinations/card IDs; and authorized broadcaster action parameters. Tracker state allows cards to resume after restart. Twitch receives channel/status requests and any authorized API actions. Credentials remain in private operator configuration.
  • OBS: the owner bound to a configured profile, any trusted-server restriction, authorized scene/input/action parameters and connection status. Authorized source screenshots are captured in memory and returned privately. Commands go to the configured OBS endpoint; another user needs their own profile/instance.
  • Profiles and saved items: optional public profile fields such as bio, pronouns, interests, favorites, time zone and birthday month/day; private bookmarks, reminders, message links and saved game-build text.
  • Community, games and staff tools: participants, moves, brackets, poll votes, giveaway entries, stories/drawing-game contributions, suggestions, kudos, configured roles/channels, moderation targets/actions/reasons/case timestamps and staff notes. Visibility depends on the selected channel/thread or private reply.
  • Music and gaming: music playback requires an exact source URL with owner-recorded audio permission, evidence, attribution and expiry; source URLs, titles, requester IDs and an in-memory queue; requested Steam store, game/news/achievement information. Source providers receive the relevant lookup or streaming request. The music feature streams playback and does not create a permanent audio archive; queues clear on restart.
  • Romance preferences: explicit fictional-romance opt-in and adult self-confirmation booleans with their update time. Nyami does not collect an ID document or full birth date for this setting. Self-confirmation is not identity verification. /chat romance enabled:False turns it off. Romantic character banter remains non-graphic and does not change filtering; all searches in this installation use Strict. Preferences do not override permissions or platform rules.

Optional cloud AI and purposes

Nyami also supports an operator-selected cloud OpenAI text backend. It will not send an individual's supplied text or selected scoped context until that person explicitly enables /nyami preferences cloud_ai:True. The versioned choice and time are recorded; older generic participation settings are insufficient. For a selected message transformation, its original human author must also have allowed cloud text sharing. Without that choice, use the local backend. Revocation cancels pending requests; data already sent remains subject to provider retention. Cloud requests use store:false, which does not guarantee zero provider retention. Uploaded-image analysis requires the local vision path and original generation uses the local renderer. Opt-in preference memory remains limited as described above.

Information is used to provide requested and enabled features, authorize actions, maintain integrations, recall conversations, avoid repeats, diagnose failures and handle support or abuse reports. Nyami does not sell personal information, run advertising targeting, or train models on Discord conversations.

Storage, safeguards and retention

Runtime databases, settings, browser state, private configuration and logs are stored on the operator's computer. The installation checks operating-system encryption for protected runtime locations and refuses startup when protection cannot be verified. Generated content and temporary video processing use encrypted local temporary storage. Data is readable in working memory during processing, and authorized operator access remains possible. These safeguards do not encrypt Discord or other providers' storage. Independent backups and recovery keys need separate protection.

  • Routine operational records: delivery history, inactive search-depth records, closed diagnostic logs and most temporary feature records use a default 30-day retention period. Maintenance runs at startup and hourly while the bot is running. Active logs and records still needed for an active integration may remain longer.
  • User-maintained information: approved AI preferences expire within 30 days. Profiles, bookmarks, builds, consent and settings remain until removed or applicable server cleanup occurs. Media permission records expire on their recorded date (1–365 days) or earlier revocation; they include the reviewing owner, purpose, basis, evidence and credit. Pending reminders remain until delivery is attempted or they are cancelled. A memory opt-out marker is kept to honor that choice.
  • Integration records: active Twitch subscriptions/settings remain while configured. Removing a tracker removes its active routing/state; bot removal clears eligible server-scoped feature, chat and delivery records after the server snapshot is reconciled. Personal cross-server records are separate. Previously posted cards/messages remain on Discord.
  • Media and browser leftovers: no new permanent search/generation media archive is created. Historical image files, older ownership/rights indexes, crash leftovers, browser history/cache and independent backups need separate operator review and cleanup. A prior media cleanup is not proof that every independent copy was deleted.

Stopping the bot does not itself erase saved records. Retention must be limited to a continuing feature, support or other applicable purpose, and the operator must address deletion requests and records no longer needed. Nyami cannot automatically delete independent backups, source-site content or other people's copies.

Access, correction, export and deletion

Use /privacy preview to count your local feature/history/memory records. /privacy export memory_page:1 returns your supported personal records privately, including up to 100 supported saved preferences per requested memory page. Further pages are requested separately. Shared staff records, other users' information, logs, browser files and independent backups are not all included in that self-service export; ask the operator for a verified request where appropriate.

/privacy delete previews and, after confirmation, removes your supported local feature/history records and shared-activity participation, clears chat memory and retains a minimal AI/memory opt-out marker so deletion does not silently re-enable participation. Removing public Discord messages, third-party copies, historical unindexed files and independent backups is a separate process.

For additional access, correction, deletion or privacy concerns, contact support privately with your Discord user ID and enough relevant server/channel/message details to locate the records. The operator may verify account ownership or server authority, while avoiding unnecessary identity documents or secrets. Requests and any applicable legal rights are handled according to the circumstances and applicable law. The operator cannot control another service's independent retention.
